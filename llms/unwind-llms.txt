# Unwind Me

> Unwind Me was an on-demand massage booking startup in San Francisco ("Massage Made Simple — straight to your door"), backed by Slow Ventures. It was acquired by Soothe (announced on unwind.me by December 2015) and shut down; unwind.me is now a parked domain. The company operates no website, developer portal, or public API today.
